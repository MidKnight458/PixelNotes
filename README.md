## ✨ Why Pixel Notes?

**Pixel Notes** is a fast, distraction-free Android note-taking studio built for students, researchers, creatives, and everyday thinkers. It pairs the fluid feel of ink on paper with the flexibility of an infinite digital whiteboard, instant PDF markup, and worry-free local and cloud backups — all wrapped in a vibrant **Material 3 Expressive** interface.

Whether you are annotating a 500-page textbook on a tablet with a stylus or sketching quick ideas on your phone, Pixel Notes stays fast, intuitive, and completely yours — **no subscriptions and no account required**.

## 🖋️ Canvas & Creative Tools

### Two Ways to Capture Ideas
| Mode | Best For | Key Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A4 Multi-Page Notebooks** | Lectures, assignments, structured notes, and PDFs | Vertical page flow, up to **15× zoom**, slide-out **Pages** panel to jump, add, or remove pages, and per-page templates (**Blank**, **Lined**, **Grid**, **Dotted**), pastel tints, or custom background images. |
| **Infinite Canvas** | Mind maps, diagrams, brainstorming, and math derivations | Boundless grid workspace with smooth multi-touch panning, up to **50× zoom**, and one-tap **Reset View** that automatically centers and frames your work. |

### Stylus-First Drawing Engine
- **Pressure-Sensitive Ink:** Tuned for active styluses including **USI 2.0**, **S-Pen**, and **Wacom EMR**, using smooth curve interpolation and high-frequency pointer sampling for natural handwriting.
- **Smart Palm Rejection:** Automatically defaults to stylus-only drawing on tablets and finger drawing on phones, with a **one-tap Finger Drawing toggle** right in the toolbar. Also supports stylus eraser tips and secondary-button quick Lasso selection.
- **Instant Shape Snapping:** Sketch a rough straight line, triangle, rectangle, pentagon, or oval and hold briefly — Pixel Notes snaps it into crisp geometry with subtle haptic feedback.
- **Pen & Multiply Highlighter:** Customize stroke widths, pick any color with the full **HSV Color Picker**, and switch rapidly using your saved recent colors. The translucent highlighter blends cleanly over ink and PDF text without washing it out.
- **Dual-Mode Eraser:** Choose between **Normal** (precision erase) and **Stroke** (erase an entire stroke in one touch), complete with adjustable size and a live visual cursor.
- **Rich Text & Custom Fonts:** Place multi-line text boxes anywhere, double-tap to edit, and style them with built-in typefaces (`Default`, `Serif`, `Monospace`, `Cursive`) or **import your own `.ttf` / `.otf` fonts** in Settings.
- **Lasso & Rectangle Selection:** Circle or box any combination of strokes, text, and images to **move, resize proportionally, recolor, change fonts, copy, duplicate, or delete** them together.
- **Floating Images:** Drop in diagrams from your gallery or snap a whiteboard photo directly from your camera — then move and scale it right beside your notes.
- **Distraction-Free Mode:** Collapse the floating toolbar in a single tap for full-screen focus, backed by full **Undo / Redo** history.

## 📄 PDF Workflow & Portable Notes

- **Instant Large-PDF Import:** Open multi-page PDFs and start writing immediately. On-demand virtual pagination keeps even huge lecture decks and textbooks responsive while saving storage space.
- **Crisp PDF Export & Batch Sharing:** Export a single note, **merge multiple selected notes into one PDF**, or share them as separate PDFs directly through Android's Share Sheet. Infinite Canvas notes automatically crop to your content with balanced margins.
- **Portable `.pxnote` Packages:** Share full, editable notes with classmates or across devices using the self-contained `.pxnote` format — preserving pages, ink, text, images, and PDF backgrounds. Tap any `.pxnote` file from your file manager or messaging app to open and import it straight into Pixel Notes.

## ☁️ Offline-First Backup & Cloud Sync

Your notes stay stored locally on your device first, so Pixel Notes works reliably anywhere — even without an internet connection.

- **Local Backups (`.zip`):** Export or restore a complete, consistent snapshot of your entire workspace — notes, folders, images, PDFs, and custom fonts — with no account required.
- **Optional Google Drive Sync:** Sign in with Google to back up your workspace into Google Drive's private App Data space (keeping your regular Drive files clutter-free).
- **Smart Non-Destructive Merge:** When signing in on a new device, Pixel Notes intelligently merges cloud notes with your local library instead of wiping existing work, while giving you full manual **Backup**, **Restore**, and **Delete Cloud Data** control.

## 🚀 Get Started

### Requirements
- **Android 10 (API 29)** or higher
- Optimized for Android phones and tablets (stylus optional, active stylus recommended for pressure sensitivity)

### Download & Install
1. Visit the [**Releases**](https://github.com/MidKnight458/PixelNotes/releases) page.
2. Download the latest `.apk` file (currently **`v1.4.2-beta`**).
3. Open the downloaded file on your Android device and allow installation from your browser/files app if prompted.

## 🛠️ For Developers

<details>
<summary><strong>Tech Stack & Building from Source</strong></summary>

### Architecture & Tech Stack
- **Language:** Kotlin (`2.2.10`)
- **UI:** Jetpack Compose, Material 3 Expressive, `androidx.graphics:graphics-shapes`
- **Architecture:** MVVM with reactive `StateFlow` & Compose state
- **Local Persistence:** Room (SQLite) with WAL snapshots (`VACUUM INTO`) & GSON converters
- **Cloud & Auth:** Android Credential Manager (Google ID) & Google Drive REST API (`appDataFolder`)
- **Media & Documents:** Native Android `PdfRenderer` / `PdfDocument`, Coil

### Build Locally
1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/MidKnight458/PixelNotes.git
   cd PixelNotes
   ```
2. Create a `secrets.properties` file in the project root (ignored by Git) if you want to enable Google Sign-In / Drive Sync:
   ```properties
   GOOGLE_WEB_CLIENT_ID=your_google_web_client_id
   ```
3. Open the project in **Android Studio** (JDK 11+, Android SDK 36) or build from the command line:
   ```bash
   ./gradlew assembleDebug
   ```
